Husbands' community

You should not have to explain all of this from the beginning.

There is a particular relief in talking with another husband who already understands the collision of clergy abuse, marriage, faith, betrayal, and secondary trauma. This community is envisioned as a confidential peer space for husbands—not a forum for blaming wives or retelling their private experiences without consent.

Be understood

Connect with men who know what it is like to support a survivor while trying to understand their own experience.

Learn together

Discuss grooming, clergy power, institutional betrayal, marriage, intimacy, parenting, faith disruption, and recovery with people who share the context.

Carry your own story

Have a place for your grief and anger that does not require your wife to become the primary container for the harm you experienced.

Community commitments: survivor-centered • confidential • no victim-blaming • no pressure to reconcile or divorce • no pressure around faith • no sharing a spouse's identifying story without permission • peer support, not therapy.
