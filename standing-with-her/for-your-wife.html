For your wife

She deserves a community that understands ACSA firsthand.

Standing With Her is being built for husbands. Your wife may also need a place where she does not have to explain the basic dynamics of adult clergy sexual abuse before she can be understood.

Restored Voices Collective is a survivor-led community of women who have experienced Adult Clergy Sexual Abuse. RVC describes its work around three needs many survivors share: connecting with other survivors, understanding their story in the context of ACSA, and reclaiming their voice.

A separate space can protect both of you. Peer support for your wife does not replace therapy or determine what choices she should make. It gives her access to women who recognize the confusion, isolation, betrayal trauma, grooming, institutional betrayal, and victim-blaming that can accompany ACSA—without requiring you to be her only source of support.

Why RVC

Built by survivors, for survivors

RVC says it was created by women who experienced sexual abuse by people who held spiritual authority and a duty of care over them. The community explicitly welcomes adult survivors who are trying to make sense of what happened and connect with others who understand.

If your wife is not ready for peer community, that choice belongs to her. You can share the resource without pressuring her to join, disclose, report, forgive, reconcile, or adopt any particular interpretation before she is ready.
