For husbands of adult clergy sexual abuse survivors

She was harmed.
You were affected, too.

When a trusted pastor or spiritual leader sexually exploits your wife, it can shake everything you thought you understood—about her experience, your marriage, your church, your faith, and yourself. You may be trying to support the person you love while carrying grief, anger, confusion, and betrayal of your own.

I just found outMeet other husbands
You are not the primary survivor

But you do not have to pretend this did not wound you.

Your wife's experience belongs to her. Your experience belongs to you. Recognizing both is not a competition over who suffered more—it can be part of caring for each of you and for the marriage.

This site is for education and peer support, not a substitute for professional mental-health, legal, or crisis services.

The idea behind this site

Your job is not to become her investigator, therapist, judge, or savior. Your job is to become a safer partner while finding safe places to carry your own pain.
